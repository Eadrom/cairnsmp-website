## Velocity Proxy with ViaVersion/ViaBackwards Support

- You no longer need one exact Minecraft version. Any Java client from **1.21 through 26.3** can join.
- The server itself runs 26.2. Older clients see newer blocks and items swapped for the closest thing their version has, so the newest version gives the best experience.

## Interactive Chat

- ShowItem plugin retired.
- Use tags in a chat message to showcase an item, your inventory, or your position. See the [command guide](/commands/#interactive-chat) for details.
- Click a player's name in chat to start a private message to them.
- Use @name to mention a player to get their attention: they hear a sound and see a note above their hotbar.

## Treasure Maps Give Loot Directly

- Buried Treasure Maps from shipwrecks and ocean ruins now give you the buried treasure loot straight away when you use them (right-click while holding).
- This replaces the outdated treasure map plugin.

## Anvil Updates

- Your anvil level cap, raised by your AuraSkills Enchanting skill perks, now applies after your Wisdom discount, so combines that only fit with the discount now go through.
- Opening an anvil tells you your current cap in chat.
- Renaming now gets the Wisdom discount too.

## Backend Updates

- Updated to the latest server and plugin builds.
- Moved Stat Gear to a new custom item backend, to support T11 and T12 crafting in future updates.
- DiscordSRV support added for an admin console view. Player chat coming in the v1.6 update.
- Fixed a bug with the new player kit: its stats now apply immediately instead of after a relog or re-equip.

## Balance Changes

### Mob Scaling

- Mobs no longer gain armor and armor toughness as they level. They keep their vanilla armor and toughness, plus any armor they spawn wearing (zombies, skeletons and the like).
- Bosses retain their defensive stat scaling.
- Overworld mob scaling is unchanged. Relative to the Overworld:
  - **Nether:** mobs scale faster with your AuraSkills power level. Mob level rises as players descend towards the bedrock floor and decreases as players ascend towards the bedrock roof. Mobs also scale faster moving away from spawn.
  - **End:** mobs get stronger the farther out you go, faster than the Overworld but slower than the Nether. Height does not affect mob level. Mob levels vary more.

### Jobs

- Jobs uses a new backend to track when a broken block should pay. You shouldn't notice a difference; if you do, please report it to the admin team.
