## Resource worlds

- Help keep the main worlds looking nice by mining, chopping, and digging to your heart's content in the new resource worlds!
- Use `/rw`, `/rw nether`, or `/rw end` to get there from anywhere.
- Once you're there, use `/tpr` to hop around and explore for loot and resources.
- They reset every Sunday at 10:30 UTC. Don't leave anything behind!
- Jobs pay 25% more and level 25% faster while you're in a resource world.
- No claims or homes there. Use `/spawn`, `/home`, or `/back` to get back.
- The main worlds now have world borders. We can push them out as we need the room.

## StatGear Crafting

- T10 gear can be upgraded to T10.5 by upgrading it to netherite at a smithing table, just like in vanilla.
- Pristine (T10) gear can now be upgraded into stronger Netherforged (T11) gear.
- Job shops sell fluxes used in the crafting, unlocked at job level 25. Check `/jobs shop`.
- Rumor has it an ancient Forgemaster left scraps of their secrets scattered around the world...
- Found a scrap? You can copy it, so share what you find with your friends.

## New Player Experience

- New players now start out in the wild, a short walk from spawn.
- The starter kit is now a full Adventurer's set: chain armor plus a stone sword, axe, and pickaxe. It's Soulbound but wears out, so it gets you to iron and then retires.
- Double skill XP for your first 30 minutes.
- New welcome messages, and helpful tips in chat every so often.

## Skill tonics

- Skill tonics can be found in structure chests. Each one doubles XP for one skill for 30 minutes.
- The timer keeps ticking down while you're offline.
- You can have several different tonics going at once, and drinking another of the same one extends its time.

## Discord chat

- Chat now goes both ways between the game and the Discord `#server-chat` channel.
- Link your accounts with `/discord link` to chat from Discord.

## Backend updates

- Updated plugins to their latest versions.
- Improvements to server security.

## Balance changes

- Sword and axe skill abilities rebalanced so axes stay competitive as slow, big hitters and swords are good for sustained damage.
- Bow Master reduced now that Sniper works properly.
- Fixed the Sniper enchant: it was lowering arrow damage instead of raising it.
- Hoes get a small damage buff, just enough to beat your fists.
- Damage-over-time enchants and the Bleed skill rebalanced for sustained damage against their exclusive instant-damage rivals (for example Venom vs Thunder on melee weapons).
- Explosive Arrows' area damage heavily reduced to bring it in line with Dragonfire Arrows.
- Rage, Temper, and Hardened rebalanced and tuned down.
- Regrowth now heals only between 10 and 20 HP, but much faster.
- Removed the Kamikadze enchant.
- Wisdom now gives 1 Max Mana per point, down from 2.
- Max Mana on Stat Gear increased from 1 to 5 per point. Existing gear keeps its old values.
- Absorption lasts longer at early ranks, but scales up more slowly.
- Lightning Blade buffed: faster attacks, cheaper, and a lower cooldown, but a shorter duration.
- Geneticist now shows the correct saturation in its tooltip, with a slight rebalance of the bonus.
- `/tpr` and `/rtp` now keep you in the dimension you're in.
