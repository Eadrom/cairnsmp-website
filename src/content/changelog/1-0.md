Cairn SMP launched on August 27, 2025, originally serving the Nevermind community. The server was tuned for relaxed, long-term play with enough progression to keep exploration interesting.

The server is now named **Cairn SMP** and its current address is `play.cairnsmp.com`.

## Economy

The optional economy system lets players earn money with jobs or chest shops, spend it with other players and shops, or buy claim blocks.

- `/balance` checks your balance.
- `/pay <name>` pays another player.
- `/buyclaimblocks <amount>` buys claim blocks at $1 per block.

## Job system

Players can take up to three jobs at a time and earn money by playing in the ways they enjoy. Browse the available jobs with `/jobs browse`.

## Chest shops

Chest shops are powered by QuickShop-Hikari.

- Buy or sell items using in-game currency.
- To create a shop, place a chest, hold an item in your active hotbar, left-click the chest, and set the price.
- Other players can use your shops while you are offline.
- Search nearby shops with `/qs finditem TO_BUY <item>`.

## Claims

Land protection is powered by GriefPrevention.

- Earn claim blocks over time or buy them directly.
- Get a golden shovel for creating and editing claims with `/kit claim`.
- The claim kit has a 10-minute cooldown.

## Teleports

- `/spawn` returns you to server spawn.
- `/home` takes you to your bed or custom home created with `/sethome`.
- `/tpask <name>` asks to teleport you to another player.
- `/tphere <name>` asks another player to teleport to you.
- `/tpaccept` accepts a pending teleport request.
- `/tpr` teleports you to a random location 500–4,000 blocks from spawn.

## Additional enchants

- ExcellentEnchants added custom enchants. View them with `/ce list`.
- Custom enchants are integrated with vanilla enchanting, loot chests, and villager trades.
- Anvil fixes removed “Too Expensive” issues while keeping XP meaningful.
- To make XP bottles, hold glass bottles and right-click an enchanting table.

## Skill system

- AuraSkills tracks player skills. View your stats with `/skills`.
- Skills level up as you play and unlock perks or mana abilities.
- On death, you lose 50% of your progress toward the current level, but never lose a completed level.
- Skill settings were adjusted to reduce grind and make abilities more impactful.

## Server tweaks

- **Difficulty:** Hard. Mobs hit harder and food matters more.
- **Mob scaling:** Tougher mobs appear farther from spawn.
- **Fire:** Fire spreads more aggressively. Only you can prevent forest fires.
- **Loot chests:** Natural chests and barrels refill every few days with new loot. Each player can loot the same chest again after one week. Breaking a chest disables refills.
- **Rotten flesh to leather:** Smelt rotten flesh in a smoker.
- **Pregenerated world:** All dimensions were pregenerated to 5,000 blocks.
- **Fishing:** Maximum wait time was reduced from 30 seconds to 20 seconds.
- **Spawners:** Spawners can be mined with a special enchant and their mob type changed with spawn eggs.
- **Spawn eggs:** Naturally spawned mobs can rarely drop spawn eggs when killed with a special weapon enchant.

The spawn welcome center includes some basic geography to help new players choose a direction, explore, and stake a claim in the world.
