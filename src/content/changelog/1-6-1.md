## Claim Flags

- You can now set a few flags on your own claims with `/setclaimflag <flag>` (and remove them with `/unsetclaimflag <flag>`):
  - `NoEnterPlayer <names>`: keep specific players out.
  - `NoEnderPearl` and `NoChorusFruit`: no teleporting into your claim.
  - `EnterMessage <text>` and `ExitMessage <text>`: greet visitors when they arrive and leave.
  - `RaidMemberOnly`: only your claim's members can start a raid there.
  - Claims are still protected from fire by default. Want fire in yours? `/unsetclaimflag NoFireDamage` lets it burn and spread there (run it again to turn protection back on).
  - `AllowBlockExplosions`: let explosions break blocks in your claim (for farms), and it stays on after restarts, unlike `/claimexplosions`.
- The [claims page](/features/claims/) has the details, including how to read `/listclaimflags`.

## Mob Levels

- Overworld mobs near spawn are gentler: levels start climbing 1,000 blocks out (was 250) and rise more slowly, so the far edge of /tpr now sees mobs around level 20 instead of the high 30s. Level 100 mobs still live inside the border for those who travel.
- The nether matches: mob levels start climbing 125 blocks out (the overworld's 1,000 divided by 8), so a portal from near spawn lands you among low-level nether mobs.
- The End's outer islands climb a little faster, so level 100 mobs (and their loot) cover a real band before the border instead of a thin edge.
- Resource worlds are easier: mob levels there climb half as fast and your power raises them half as much.
- Creepers level 20 and up blast a bigger area and hit much harder. Kill them before they go off!

## Loot

- 26.3's new abandoned camps can hold XP tonics and Forgemaster's scraps, like other structure chests. The hidden chest is the better find.
- Some wandering traders now carry a single XP tonic for 64 emeralds. Catch him while you can!
