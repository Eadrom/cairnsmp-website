- The server now runs Minecraft 26.3. Any Java client from **1.21 through 26.3** can still join, but 26.3 gives the best experience.

## Claim Flags

- You can now set a few flags on your own claims with `/setclaimflag <flag>`, and remove them with `/unsetclaimflag <flag>`:
  - `NoEnterPlayer <names>`: keep specific players out.
  - `NoEnderPearl` and `NoChorusFruit`: no teleporting into your claim.
  - `EnterMessage <text>` and `ExitMessage <text>`: greet visitors when they arrive and leave.
  - `RaidMemberOnly`: only your claim's members can start a raid there.
  - `AllowBlockExplosions`: let explosions break blocks in your claim for farms that need it. Unlike `/claimexplosions`, it stays on after a restart.
- Claims are still protected from fire. Want fire in yours? `/unsetclaimflag NoFireDamage` toggles it.
- See the [claims page](/features/claims/) for details, including how to read `/listclaimflags`.

## Skill Tonics

- Skill tonics can now turn up in the chests of 26.3's new abandoned camps. The hidden chest is the better find, and some say the Forgemaster passed through there too...
- Some wandering traders now carry a single skill tonic for 64 emeralds. Catch them while you can!

## Backend Updates

- Updated the server and plugins to the latest versions for 26.3.

## Balance Changes

### Mob Scaling

- **Overworld:** the area around spawn is now a calm zone. Mob levels start climbing 1,000 blocks out instead of 250, and climb more slowly after that, so the edge of `/tpr` now sees mobs around level 20 instead of the high 30s. Level 100 mobs are still out there for those who travel.
- **Nether:** matches the Overworld, so a portal from near spawn lands you among low-level mobs.
- **End:** the outer islands climb a little faster, so the toughest mobs (and their loot) now fill a real band before the border instead of a thin edge.
- **Resource worlds:** a good bit easier than the main worlds. Mob levels climb half as fast, and your power level raises them half as much.
- **Creepers** level 20 and up now have a bigger blast that hits much harder. Deal with them before they go off!
- Bats no longer spawn.
