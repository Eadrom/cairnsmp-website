## Server and Plugins Updates

- The server now runs Minecraft 26.3. Any Java client from **1.21 through 26.3** can still join, but 26.3 gives the best experience.

## Claim Flags

- You can now set a few flags to customize your claims with GriefPrevention's Claim Flags
- See the [claims page](/features/claims/) for details on what flags are available and how to use them.

## Balance Changes

- You can find skill tonics for sale by some wandering traders and in some of the new abandoned camps
- Resource world monsters now scale much more slowly
- The persistent Overworld has a 1000 block radius buffer before monster scaling starts (with a corresponding match in the Nether)
- Monster level also now scales slower in the Overworld and Nether, providing a less intensive experience for new players
- In the End, monsters scale slightly faster to provide a larger band of level 100 mobs to farm.
- Creepers over level 20 now have a more threatening explosion.
