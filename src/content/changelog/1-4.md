## Server name change

- Nevermind SMP is now **Cairn SMP**.
- The new server address is `play.cairnsmp.com`.
- The rebrand creates room to welcome more regular players alongside the friends and guildmates who play seasonally.
- Cairn SMP remains first and foremost a server by Eadrom for friends, Nevermind guildmates, and friends-of-friends.
- Nothing was reset. Builds, claims, inventories, and player progress remain.
- Players should log in at least once a year to keep claims active.
- Work began on this website to provide changelogs, commands, and a lightweight player handbook.

:::note[What is a cairn?]
A cairn is a human-made stack of stones traditionally used as a landmark, trail marker, memorial, or waypoint.
:::

## New feature: Stat Gear

- Stat Gear grants AuraSkills stats such as Strength and Luck and can rarely drop from hostile mobs in all three dimensions.
- Tools and weapons are mob-themed. Armor affixes are biome-themed, with rare biomes offering stronger affixes.
- Gear scales across 10 bands from level 1–100. Higher-level mobs drop better gear.
- Bosses always drop one level 100 item from a special boss pool, with a small chance to drop a second item.

## Server and mob scaling

- Updated server software and plugins. This release requires **Minecraft Java Edition 26.2**.
- Hostile mobs now scale to level 100 based on AuraSkills power and distance from spawn.
- Levelled Mobs moved to Gold Challenge. Mobs below level 50 are weaker, making the area near spawn less dangerous.
- Mob-name colors now match Stat Gear quality using familiar rarity colors.
- Wardens, Withers, and Ender Dragons always spawn at level 100. Bring friends and come prepared.

## AuraSkills balance

- Strength damage decreased from 0.4% to 0.25% per point.
- Toughness damage reduction increased from 0.3% to 0.34% per point.
- Enchanted Strength decreased from 0.5 to 0.25 per enchant level.
- Axe Master, Sword Master, Bow Master, First Strike, and Bleed were reduced in power.
- Lightning Blade was redesigned, and ability progression was rebalanced to provide more benefit earlier.
- Shielding and Immunity received small improvements.
