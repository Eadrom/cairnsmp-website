## New features

- A new plugin lets players share item links in chat. Use `/showitem` to show the item in your hand. To show a shield, leave your active hotbar slot empty.
- The MasterCutter datapack adds hundreds of stonecutter recipes. These include converting Stone or Deepslate into their Cobbled variants and quality-of-life recipes such as stripping an entire stack of logs at once. [View MasterCutter on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/mastercutter).

## Teleports

- Fixed `/tprandom` so it correctly chooses a random location in whichever of the three dimensions the player is currently in, rather than always using the Overworld.
- Reduced the teleport delay from five seconds to three seconds.

## Claims

- Claims now extend 128 blocks downward by default. They continue to expand downward automatically as the claim owner builds or digs.
- Reduced the minimum claim width to four blocks and minimum area to 16 blocks (4×4). This is the smallest size that clearly displays all four claim corners.
- Loosened piston restrictions. Pistons can now be used anywhere provided the piston, surrounding area, and moved blocks do not overlap another player’s claim. This retains the majority of piston-related grief protection while remaining performant.

## Enchants and balance

- Confirmed that the Silk Spawner enchant does not affect trial spawners.
- Dramatically reduced bonus Fishing skill XP from rare and epic loot. These catches had been granting entire levels to lower-level players; they remain rewarding without providing disproportionate progression.
- Rebalanced several ExcellentEnchants effects for heavily enchanted items. Some activation chances and proc damage were reduced, while debuffs were made less oppressive.
- Removed ExcellentEnchants curses from villager trades. Curses should now appear only on mob and treasure loot.

## Maintenance

- Updated server software and plugins.
