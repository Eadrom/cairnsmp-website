title: Roadmap
description: What’s coming to Cairn SMP. Plans, not promises.
---

Here’s what the admin team is working on next, roughly in the order we expect to get to it. Nothing here has a date, and the order and details can change. For what has already shipped, see the [changelog](/changelog/).

1. **Legendary gear.** A further upgrade tier beyond Netherforged gear.
2. **A shared server market space** with stalls you can rent for your shop.
3. **A PvP arena.**
4. **Hardcore seasons.** A seasonal hardcore version of Cairn running for one to three months, with rewards for your main Cairn character based on what you accomplish during the season.

Have an idea, or a favorite on this list? Share it in the [Cairn SMP Discord](https://discord.gg/6p8UXyXMzr).
