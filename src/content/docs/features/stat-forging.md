Stat Forging takes your best [Stat Gear](/features/stat-gear/) past Pristine (T10).

## T10.5

Upgrade a Pristine diamond item to netherite at a smithing table, just like in vanilla. It keeps its stats.

## Netherforged (T11)

A Netherforged item is crafted from four parts:

- **A piece of netherite armor, tool, or weapon.** Items that don't have a netherite version need a netherite ingot as well in the final recipe.
- **A Power Core.** Only bosses possess the powerful artifacts and materials needed to fuel the creation of such powerful gear.
- **An Affix Sigil.** Disenchant a piece of T10 gear to extract its sigil. The type of sigil decides the stats and traits the crafted item will have.
- **A Catalyst.** Fueling the crafting process, a catalyst applies a sigil to a core and binds its magical properties to your gear.

Netherforged gear gets a stronger primary stat and primary trait.

## Essences, fluxes and sigils

- **Essences** are crafted from big stacks of themed materials. The resource worlds are the place to gather them.
- **Fluxes** are sold in job shops from job level 25 (`/jobs shop`). Can't find anyone with the right job? Every job's shop sells a **Universal Flux** from level 30, and it works in any catalyst recipe.
- Don't have the sigil you want? You can **transmute** one sigil into another.

## The Netherforge Codex

Rumor has it an ancient Forgemaster left scraps of their secrets in chests around the world. Gather them all to restore the Netherforge Codex, which holds every Stat Forging recipe. Scraps can be copied, so share what you find with your friends.
