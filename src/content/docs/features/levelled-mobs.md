Hostile mobs on Cairn SMP have levels from 1 to 100. A mob’s level and its name color appear above its head.

## How levels work

- **Distance from spawn.** Mobs get tougher the farther you travel from spawn.
- **Your power.** Levels also rise with your AuraSkills power level, so the world keeps pace as you grow.
- **Depth.** In the Overworld and the Nether, mobs get tougher the deeper you go.

Levels add health and damage, not armor. Mobs keep their vanilla armor and toughness, plus any armor they spawn wearing.

## Each dimension

- **Overworld:** distance, power and depth, as above.
- **Nether:** levels climb faster with your power and with distance from spawn. Depth matters twice as much: mob level rises as you descend toward the bedrock floor and drops as you climb toward the bedrock roof.
- **End:** the main island scales only with your power. In the outer islands, mobs get stronger the farther out you go, faster than the Overworld but slower than the Nether. Height doesn't matter, and levels vary more from mob to mob.

The server runs on **Hard** difficulty, so even low-level mobs hit harder and food matters more.

## Name colors

Mob name colors use the same quality colors as [Stat Gear](/features/stat-gear/), from gray at the low end to purple at the top. A mob’s color hints at the band of gear it can drop.

## Bosses

Wardens, Withers and Ender Dragons always spawn at **level 100**, and unlike other mobs they keep their extra armor from levels. Bring friends and come prepared.

Every boss kill drops one level-100 item from a special boss loot table, with a small chance of a second.

## Rewards

Higher-level mobs drop better Stat Gear. Drops come only from mobs killed by players, and mobs from spawners don’t drop Stat Gear.
