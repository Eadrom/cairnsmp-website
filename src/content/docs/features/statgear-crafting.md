StatGear Crafting takes your best [Stat Gear](/features/stat-gear/) past Pristine (T10). Recipes aren't listed here: figuring them out is part of the fun.

## T10.5

Upgrade a Pristine diamond item to netherite at a smithing table, just like in vanilla. It keeps its stats.

## Netherforged (T11)

A Netherforged item is crafted from four parts:

- **A netherite item** to forge it on.
- **A Power Core**, made from a core that only a boss can give you.
- **An Affix Sigil**, broken down from a T10 item. It decides the item's affix.
- **A Catalyst**, crafted from that affix's flux and essences.

Netherforged gear gets a stronger primary stat and primary trait.

## Essences, fluxes and sigils

- **Essences** are crafted from big stacks of themed materials. The resource worlds are the place to gather them.
- **Fluxes** are sold in job shops from job level 25 (`/jobs shop`).
- Don't have the sigil you want? You can **transmute** one sigil into another.

## The Forgemaster's Codex

Rumor has it an ancient Forgemaster left scraps of their secrets in chests around the world. Gather them all to restore the Forgemaster's Codex, which holds every recipe. Scraps can be copied, so share what you find with your friends.
