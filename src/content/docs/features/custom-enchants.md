Cairn SMP adds dozens of custom enchantments through ExcellentEnchants. Browse them in game with `/ce list`, which shows each enchant and the ones it excludes.

## Finding enchantments

Custom enchants come from the same places as vanilla ones:

- the enchanting table
- loot chests
- villager trades
- gear worn by mobs

Curses turn up on mob and treasure loot, never on villager trades.

Some enchantments open doors the vanilla game keeps shut. Spawners can be mined with the right enchant, and the right weapon enchant can make naturally spawned mobs rarely drop their spawn eggs. Finding out which enchant does what is part of the game.

## Exclusive groups

Many enchants come in exclusive sets: gear can carry only one from each set. For example, a weapon can have one of Temper, Rage or Double Strike, and arrows can be Poisoned or Electrified, not both. `/ce list` shows the exclusions.

Fewer enchants per item means each one matters more.

## Anvils

- Combining heavily enchanted gear is possible: the Enchanting skill raises the “Too Expensive” cap, and the Wisdom stat discounts anvil costs. See [AuraSkills](/features/auraskills/#anvil-master).
- The anvil shows the real cost of a combination, including those discounts.

## XP bottles

Hold glass bottles and right-click an enchanting table to store your experience in XP bottles.
